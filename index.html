Cheetah Breedah

Can you breed a King Cheetah?

Raise and care for real personalities

Feed them, exercise them, play with them, and keep their space clean. Every cat has its own personality, its own build, and its own needs, and neglecting them has real consequences.

Feel like a real scientist

Certain information about your cats is locked behind things like number of interactions, time spent observed, and time spent with other animals. Use every option to learn everything you can, or just watch them play.

Watch them actually live in their space

Cheetahs move around and pose based on their actual dispositions. Watch them mingle, or toss a ball and watch them chase it down and pounce on it.

Dive into real genetics

The game tracks dozens of genetic traits and actual kinship between your animals. Breed two cheetahs that are too closely related and you'll get a warning, with real consequences for the litter if you go ahead anyway.

Mingle Mode

Provide basic care for your whole roster at once and watch them interact as a group. Observation time actually means something in this game.

Build a roster worth being proud of

No two cheetahs raise the same, so keep building up your studbook and make breeding choices that actually mean something.

Regular or Casual: Regular mode lets days pass in real time. Casual mode ignores days you don't open the app. Play Casual if you just want a digital pet, or Regular if you want to manage a small zoo.

What's the goal?

Some cheetahs are born with a striped pattern on their back instead of the typical spots. These are called King Cheetahs. In 2012, it was discovered that this is caused by a recessive gene carried by both parents, about a 1 in 1,000 chance, with only around 10 estimated living in the wild today. The main goal of this game, other than being a silly little cat game, is to try and purposely breed a King Cheetah using simulated lab tests and real-world-inspired cheetah husbandry techniques.

Mostly it's just a silly little cat game, though.

FAQ

The Google Play link says the app isn't available, or the page won't load. What's wrong?

You're not in the tester group yet. Google Play only shows a closed testing or open beta page to accounts that have joined the linked group first. Join the tester group, then reopen the testing link using the same Google account.

I joined the group. Now what?

Come back to this page and tap the testing link for whichever app you want to try. It can take a few minutes after joining before Google Play recognizes you as a member, so give it a moment if it still doesn't load right away.

Do I need a separate sign-up for each app?

No. One tester group membership covers every app on this page, plus anything new that goes into testing later.
